# development

- static browser game with vendored three.js r160 in `vendor/three/`; no runtime build step.
- run `python3 devserver.py` from this folder for the no-cache server on port 5179.
- check changed javascript with `node --check <file>` and run `git diff --check`.
- verify in a browser. local-only `window.__rawah` hooks (`begin`, `goto`, `teleport`, `setHour`, `look`, `press`, `hold`, `step`, `map`, `travel(name)`, `weather(regime)`) drive deterministic checks when pointer lock is unavailable; `step(n)` only renders the last frame. keep the hooks disabled on public hosts.
- world data in `data/` is generated by `tools/bake_world.py` from real sources (USGS lidar, Sentinel-2, Overture/OpenStreetMap); edit the script and re-bake rather than hand-editing `world.json` or the binaries. downloads and expensive rasters cache in `tools/.cache/`.
- `World.height()` is lidar (`baseHeight`, quad split along the (c, r)-(c+1, r+1) diagonal) plus micro-relief from `src/detail.js`; the CDLOD vertex shader computes the same thing. keep the JS and GLSL halves of `detail.js` in lock-step (same hashing, octaves, offsets, weights) or the hiker will float or sink.
- `World.cover()` turns the baked Sentinel-2 land cover (live conifer, burned, aspen, rock) plus terrain into the weights flora uses; keep tree placement driven by it so trees match the satellite colour underneath.
- names come only from OpenStreetMap data; do not invent names for unnamed lakes or features.
- all sound is synthesized in `src/audio.js`; no audio files.
- trees are generated at startup in `src/trees.js` (painted atlas, card meshes, impostor bake); flora placement stays in `src/flora.js`.
- camera feel lives in `src/camera.js` and is driven by `Hiker.phase` (PI per footstep); keep footstep audio and head bob on that same phase. `V` toggles first/third person; `src/avatar.js` is a placeholder meant to be replaced by a rigged model.
- weather lives in `src/weather.js` as a few numbers (cloud, thick, precip, fog, wind, temp, storm) eased toward the day's seeded schedule; everything else reads them. cloud density has one GLSL source (`CLOUD_GLSL` in `src/atmosphere.js`) used by the sky and the ground's cloud shadows, plus a two-octave JS twin (`cloudDensityAt`); keep them in step.
- materials join the shared light and weather through `withAtmosphere(shader, { translucency, weather })`; don't fork per-material fog, cloud shadows, wetness or snow.
- gamepad input uses the standard mapping (what an Xbox controller reports). game actions go through the same key codes as the keyboard (`PAD` in `src/input.js`); menus and the map take named buttons via `Input.onPad`.
- the date lives in `src/calendar.js`; seasonal looks (leaves, grass curing, snowpack, lake ice, meadow green-up) come from it, not from hard-coded months elsewhere. the season is a setting and changing it reloads, because the tree atlas and impostors are painted for the season at boot.
- UI type: Inter for interface text, Fraunces for names and titles (vendored in `fonts/`). prompts are written as `[Key]` and `hud.js` renders them as keycaps, Xbox buttons or touch labels for the active input.
- culling lives in `src/culling.js`: `HorizonMap` (terrain skyline around the camera, for occlusion of CDLOD nodes, flora tiles and impostors, trail/stream chunks), `ChunkSet`/`chunkGeometry` (huge static meshes cut into rejectable chunks), and the expanded frustum used by `flora.js` when it refills its instance buffers. grass is drawn in blocks that are frustum-tested in `grass.js`. flora keeps every shadow caster when the sun is low (long shadows), and sweeps shadows otherwise. in dev, `__rawah.CULL.frustum / occlusion` switch it off for A/B pixel comparisons (freeze the clock with `state.paused`, hide skinned meshes, and set `renderer.toneMappingExposure` before each render or the eye adaptation will drift the images).
- the four-wheeler lives in `src/atv.js` (arcade driving, four-point suspension, water/slope/tree limits); `main.js` handles mounting with R, the chase camera and the rider pose. it uses `assets/vehicles/atv.glb` if present (fitted by `MODEL` in atv.js: length, and `flip` if the nose is backwards) and a procedural stand-in otherwise.
- fishing is `src/fishing.js`: [T] casts at open lake water ahead, Space hooks the bite and reels (held), A/D or a mouse swing leans against the fish's pull. Rod, line, bobber, ripples and fish are scene objects; `main.js` locks walking while a fish is on. species are chosen by lake elevation (SPECIES in fishing.js); the catch log is saved with the game and shown in the journal. dev: `__rawah.fishing`.
- the night sky is `src/stars.js`: ~16k stars as points plus a Milky Way baked once into a cube map in galactic coordinates (bulge, star clouds, Great Rift, dark nebulae), both turned by real local sidereal time (`siderealAngle`) at the trailhead latitude. GLSL gotchas hit here: `patch` is a reserved word, and `pow` with a negative base gives NaN on Apple GPUs (use a square). custom ShaderMaterials must include the `logdepthbuf_*` chunks (the renderer uses a logarithmic depth buffer) or they fail the depth test against everything else. the moon setting (new/quarter/full) is in `sky.js` `MOONS`.
- camp: `camp.js` pitches the tent and, with the fire, two stools; `fire.js` is the fire (logs, coals, flame/spark/smoke sprites animated in the vertex shader, flickering light); [R] sits on a free stool, Grant takes the other; `roast.js` is the marshmallow ([Q] to take one, hold Space over the flames, [Q] to eat). Settings has a time-of-day jump and the moon phase.
- the camp's fun: while you roast a marshmallow near the tent, Grant sometimes asks (a yes/no card, [Y]/[N] or pad A/B); yes starts a scripted scene in `main.js` (`startScene`/`cutStep`): both walk to the tent door, vanish, `camp.shake(10)` rocks the tent, they come out. flames are procedural strips in `fire.js` (the fire is built at `UNIT` 0.68 of full size); grass is cleared around the fire and tent with `grass.clear`.
- firewood is `src/wood.js`: [X] (held) chops the choppable tree you face (`flora.treeAt` / `flora.fell`, which also leaves a stump and remembers the tree so it stays felled); the real tree geometry falls about its stump until the crown first touches the ground (`#contact`), then settles onto the two highest supports either side of its centre of mass (`#rest`, an upper convex hull of the ground under it) so it bridges dips and follows slopes; it lands whole, crown and all, and the bare trunk wears the same atlas bark strip (`barkUV`) so it doesn't change colour; the first saw stroke takes the branches off, leaving one continuous trunk mesh that shortens as holding [X] saws fire-log lengths off the thick end, each flying to a pyramid stack; the grass is cleared a foot either side of the trunk only (`grass.clear` takes strips as well as circles); [B] drops the pack as a neat stack (re-takeable with [R]); [R] takes an armful (pack of 16) or stacks the pack in the camp woodpile; [F] at the fire lays a log (+60 min), and lighting a fire costs three logs from the pack or camp pile. unfinished trunks and ground stacks are not saved; the pack, the camp pile and the felled trees are.
